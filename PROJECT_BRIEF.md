# Camp Walden Kitchen App MVP

## Purpose

Provide one operational platform for Camp Walden food service.

The app manages:

- Menu planning
- Meal production planning
- Vendor product references
- Order planning
- Prep operations
- Domestic staff scheduling
- International staff scheduling

## What this is not

- No live inventory
- No POS
- No sales tracking
- No food-cost dashboard MVP
- No Sysco integration

## Core workflow

Menu
-> Meal Production Report
-> Order Builder
-> Vendor-specific purchase sheets

## Menu management

Support:

- Calendar view
- Spreadsheet view
- Day view

Allow:

- Add item
- Remove item
- Replace item
- Drag/drop when practical
- Undo
- Draft menus
- Published menus
- Printable public menus

## Public menu

Camper/staff-facing menu.

Simple presentation with no SKU or vendor details.

## Internal production menu

Each menu item may be linked to:

- Preferred vendor product
- Alternate vendor products
- SKU/product number
- Pack size
- Case quantity
- Servings per case
- Suggested quantity
- Production notes

## Product library

Maintain a searchable product reference database built from historical Van Eerden and GFS invoices.

A menu item can be connected to multiple approved products.

## Meal production reports

Example:

Spaghetti Dinner
Expected Count: 425

Required Products:

- VE#93928 Stanislaus Tomato Product - 6 cans
- Ground Beef - quantity based on serving assumptions
- Garlic Bread - quantity based on serving assumptions

Reports should be editable.

## Scheduling

Domestic Schedule:

- Traditional weekly scheduling
- Hours and assignments

International Schedule:

- Assignment schedule
- Break schedule

Keep these as separate modules.

## Ordering

Side-by-side workflow:

Left:
- Menu week
- Meal reports

Right:
- Suggested order quantities
- Vendor products
- Export purchase sheets

Exports should support:

- GFS
- Van Eerden

## Prep operations

Support:

- Breakfast bar checklists
- Salad bar checklists
- Daily prep lists
- Completion tracking

## Branding

Camp Walden branding.

Modern operations platform.

Not a restaurant POS aesthetic.
